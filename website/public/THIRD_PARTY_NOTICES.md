## Fonts

The self-hosted Chinese fonts are subsets of LXGW WenKai Regular and Medium,
revision `048a6a97c7f354437670d6b92276073cd75e8a5d`, under SIL OFL 1.1.
The complete copyright and license are in `public/assets/fonts/OFL.txt`.
The subset family name is Hasp WenKai Subset. Charter is used only when
already installed on the reader's device; no Charter font file is distributed.
Kami's commercial TsangerJinKai font is not bundled.

## Imagery

Hasp icons belong to this project. Panel images render the real production
SwiftUI components with synthetic fixture data, using `scripts/render-assets.swift`.
